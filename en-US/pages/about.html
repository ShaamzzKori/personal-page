About me

IT support is not only tickets. It is people who need a printer to work right now, and infrastructure that must not fall over mid-shift.

How I got into IT

I started in 2015 taking calls in a support queue: figuring out what exactly had broken on the other end of the line and passing it on to engineers. Six months later I moved to dispatching work at MAYKOR, where I first saw support as a process — deadlines, SLA, spare parts, reporting.

Since December 2017 I work directly with hardware: first the Danone plant in Yekaterinburg, and since 2019 Nutricia (Danone) in Istra. Second line, manufacturing systems, servers, network equipment, antivirus hygiene.

How I work

  • Reproduce first. Until a problem reproduces, any fix is a guess. Diagnostics is cheaper than "reinstall and see".
  • Fix it so it does not come back. Replacing components and configuring software is a chance to find the cause, not only the symptom.
  • Leave instructions. After a difficult case there is a short document, so next time anyone can handle it — including me in six months.
