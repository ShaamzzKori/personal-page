About me

I started in 2015 taking calls in a service desk queue, then moved to dispatching field engineers, and since 2017 I have worked directly in IT support at manufacturing sites: Danone in Yekaterinburg, then Nutricia in Istra.

Along the way I settled on a simple rule: reproduce the problem first, then fix it — and always leave an instruction behind, so the next person can handle it.
